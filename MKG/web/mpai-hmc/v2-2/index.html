Technical Specification: Human and Machine Communication (MPAI-HMC) V2.2 enables an Entity – a human in an audio-visual scene of a real space or a Machine represented in an Audio-Visual Scene of a Virtual Space as a Virtual Human – to hold a multi-modal communication with another Entity possibly in a different Context – such as language and culture – in a way that is agnostic of the Entities’ real or virtual nature.
MPAI-HMC V2.2 is published with a request for Community Comments. Please send your comments to the MPAI Secretariat by 2025/10/26.
