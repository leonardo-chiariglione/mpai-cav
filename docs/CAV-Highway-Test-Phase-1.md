# CAV reference software - test phase 1: highway overtaking

MPAI document M3253 - specification of the synthetic test scenario, draft, 2026-10-08.

## 1. Purpose

Test the CAV reference software in a first closed-loop scenario in which the truth is known. The CAV keeps its planned speed, overtakes slower vehicles, and watches for faster ones. The sequences are generated by software, and each run is repeatable from a seed.

## 2. The scenario

### 2.1 The road

A straight divided highway. Only the carriageway the CAV travels on is considered: two lanes, one direction. The left lane is the overtaking lane. A guard rail on the left hides the opposite carriageway, and there is a paved shoulder on the right. Lanes are 3.5 m wide, with solid edge lines and a dashed line between the two lanes.

### 2.2 The other traffic

- Cars and trucks. Each driver has a desired speed: about 112 km/h for cars in the right lane, 130 km/h for cars in the left lane, and 88 km/h for trucks, each with a spread.
- A driver's speed varies randomly: a slow drift, and occasional slowdowns.
- Drivers follow the vehicle ahead (Intelligent Driver Model) and change lanes as drivers do: left to pass a slower vehicle, back to the right when it is free. They do not collide with each other, and they brake if the CAV cuts in front of them.
- The traffic density (vehicles an hour in each lane) is a setting. Everything follows from the seed.

### 2.3 The CAV

The ego vehicle starts in the right lane with a planned speed. The planned speed is a setting and never exceeds the limit.

## 3. The CAV's sensors

Capture is always at full resolution: 1920 x 1080 on all four cameras.

| Cameras | Spacing | Horizontal view | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front, two (stereo pair) | 0.30 m | 60 degrees | Lane markings, vehicles ahead, distance by stereo |
| Rear, two (stereo pair) | 0.30 m | 40 degrees | Long range: vehicles approaching in the left lane |

- The configuration can reduce each camera's resolution by a factor (1, 2, 3 and so on). By default the detector works from a 640 pixel wide image. Full resolution crops are used only when needed: a far search ahead, or the check behind just before a lane change.
- GNSS and ego motion remain as they are today.

Limits for now: a blind spot beside the car (closer than about 10 m), no LiDAR or radar, a flat road, daytime and clear weather.

## 4. How the CAV should behave

1. Cruise at the planned speed in the right lane.
2. When a slower vehicle ahead comes within the decision distance, look behind in the left lane, and also ahead in it.
3. Would a vehicle behind in the left lane have to brake harder than a limit (default 3 m/s2) if the CAV pulled out? If so, wait and follow at a safe gap, then check again.
4. If the lane is clear, move left over about 4 seconds. Speed up during the pass, never above the limit, so that the time spent in the left lane stays under a maximum (default 12 seconds).
5. Return to the right lane once safely past the slower vehicle, and only if the right lane has room.
6. Abort the manoeuvre if a fast vehicle appears behind, or if the vehicle ahead in the left lane brakes.

Today's Autonomous Motion Subsystem only follows the vehicle ahead and keeps its lane (Path Selection Planning stage 1: "lane changes are a later stage"). Steps 2 to 6 are new work.

## 5. What is measured in each run

- **Safety:** collisions; minimum gap and time gap; the hardest braking the CAV forced on other drivers.
- **Task:** overtakes completed; time spent in the left lane; average speed against the planned speed.
- **Comfort:** maximum lateral acceleration and jerk.
- **Rules:** overtake on the left, return to the right.
- **Perception:** detections against the truth by distance; stereo distance error; time taken by a step.

## 6. Scenarios

| Code | Description |
|---|---|
| S0 | Empty road. |
| S1 | One slow vehicle ahead. |
| S2 | A slow vehicle ahead and a fast one approaching in the left lane: the CAV must wait. |
| S3 | A platoon of slow vehicles. |
| S4 | Dense traffic with no gap. |
| S5 | Random traffic, seeds 1 to 100, with statistics over all the runs. |

## 7. Build order and status

1. Road and traffic generator - written, not yet tested.
2. The four-camera model, with tests and sample images.
3. A baseline run with today's Autonomous Motion Subsystem (expected: it gets stuck behind slow vehicles; the run measures by how much).
4. Rear sensing in the Environment Sensing Subsystem.
5. The lane-change planning.
6. A batch runner and a report in the style of the existing test reports.

## 8. Assumptions and open points

- Rear detection must reach about 95 m for a vehicle closing at 11 m/s, and about 190 m at 20 m/s (assuming a braking limit of 3 m/s2, a 20 m minimum gap and 5 s for the manoeuvre and the reaction).
- Stereo distance error is about 1.3 m at 50 m, 5 m at 100 m and 11 m at 150 m (assuming a quarter pixel matching precision).
- Still to set: the planned speed, the decision distance, the pass/fail thresholds, the exact list of sensors the MPAI-CAV specification expects, and where the batch of runs executes.
