The AIF: the AI environment

The AI Framework is a layer above the operating system. MPAI Apps depend on it and not on Windows, Linux or the phone you hold. Choose your environment.

Windows

available

The AIF for Windows 10 and 11 on 64-bit Intel and AMD processors.

Linux

in preparation

The same Controller, in a package for Linux. The L3s and the Apps do not change; the packages of the AIMs and the programs they use are made for Linux.

macOS

planned

The same Controller, in a package for macOS. The L3s and the Apps do not change.

Android

planned

A phone or a tablet holds the microphone, the camera and the screen. The Apps run on an MPAI-MAS Service and the access program is its client.

iOS

planned

An iPhone or an iPad holds the microphone, the camera and the screen. The Apps run on an MPAI-MAS Service and the access program is its client.

What you get

  • An environment where AI Modules run under a Controller, with their data and their trust looked after.
  • Access to the MPAI Store: the Apps and AI Modules it has approved, checked again each time they are loaded.
  • One program, MPAI Home (provisional name), to reach the world of MPAI Apps.
