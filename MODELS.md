# Models and data used by the software

This page lists every model and every piece of data that the software obtains from a public source: where it comes from (a link), its SHA-256, its licence (a link) and whether it concerns personal data. The list is made from the `AccessSources` of the AIM Metadata (`AIMs/AMDs`) and is checked by the tests: a model that is declared and not listed makes a test fail. The same list is in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, with the other files made by others.
